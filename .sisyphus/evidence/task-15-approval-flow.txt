Reuse contract with T21 (useToolApproval)
------------------------------------------
The dispatcher NEVER imports `useToolApproval`. The orchestrator construction
site (T18 prompt bar) is responsible for wiring `deps.approvalGate` to:

    approvalGate: (toolName, threadId, params) =>
        useToolApproval.getState().showApprovalModal(toolName, threadId, params)

`showApprovalModal` already auto-resolves `true` for:
  - `allowAllMCPPermissions === true`
  - `isToolApproved(threadId, toolName) === true`

So the dispatcher's two-line "consult the gate" block transparently honours
the user's existing T21 settings — no policy duplication.
